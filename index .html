FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services, appointments, and visiting Hallel Hospital.

While we accept walk-ins, we highly recommend booking an appointment to reduce your waiting time. You can easily book online through our website or call us directly. However, for emergencies, please come in immediately—no appointment is needed.

Expecting mothers should bring a valid ID, their antenatal card, comfortable clothing, baby clothes, diapers, and toiletries. If you have an HMO, please bring your HMO card as well. Our team will provide a detailed checklist during your antenatal visits.

We accept major HMOs including Bastion, AXA Mansard, Reliance, Avon, and more. You can verify your coverage by calling our front desk at +234 704 288 2756 or using the chat feature on our website. Please have your HMO card or policy number ready.

Yes! Hallel Hospital is open 24/7, 365 days a year, including all public holidays and weekends. Our emergency department is always fully staffed to handle urgent medical situations.

We accept cash, bank transfers, POS (debit/credit cards), and approved HMO plans. Payment is typically required after consultation or before procedures, but emergencies are treated first before financial discussions.
